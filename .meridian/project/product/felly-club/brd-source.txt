1. Executive Summary
Felly Club is a curated, tech-enabled mentorship marketplace designed to connect ambitious individuals (mentees) with accomplished, domain-verified mentors across Sport, Entrepreneurship, Business, and Entertainment. The platform enables mentees to discover, book, and attend one-on-one video mentorship sessions, while giving mentors a structured channel to monetize their expertise.
Nagarro has been engaged to design and deliver the end-to-end digital platform — web application, mobile application, and administration portal — under a fixed-price engagement of €125,000. Delivery follows an agile model across eight sprints (approximately 16 weeks).

2. Business Context & Problem Statement
2.1 Vision
"To establish Felly Club as the premier tech-enabled mentorship ecosystem, empowering individuals to connect with accomplished mentors, gain personalized guidance, and accelerate progress in their chosen fields. By leveraging advanced analytics and fostering trust, the platform delivers meaningful mentor–mentee experiences and builds a scalable community that supports long-term personal and professional growth."
Share  |  Connect  |  Grow
2.2 Problem Statement
The global mentorship landscape is fragmented and opaque. Aspiring individuals struggle to identify credible, accessible mentors, while accomplished professionals lack a structured, trustworthy channel to offer guidance. Key pain points include:
	•	No standardised discovery mechanism for domain-specific mentors
	•	Absence of transparent pricing, availability, and quality indicators
	•	High friction in scheduling, payment, and session delivery
	•	Limited accountability and dispute resolution mechanisms
	•	No data-driven insights for either mentors or mentees

7. Technical Architecture & Technology Stack
7.1 Architecture Overview
The Felly Club platform follows a modular, cloud-native architecture with clear separation of concerns across the following layers:
Layer
Description
Presentation Layer
Next.js/React web app (Vercel-hosted) + React Native iOS/Android apps. Shared design system and API contracts ensure feature parity.
Identity & Access Management
KeyCloak for centralised authentication, role-based access, and social login. API Gateway enforces JWT validation, CORS, routing, and rate limiting.
Headless CMS
Strapi decouples content from presentation via REST/GraphQL APIs, enabling rapid content updates for FAQs, marketing pages, and dynamic in-app content.
Service Layer
Modular Node.js services handling: users, sessions, meetings, payments, search, reviews, and notifications. Designed for independent scalability.
Data Layer
PostgreSQL (transactional data), Redis (caching, session, search results), Blob Storage (media assets, session recordings).
Third-Party Integrations
Zoom (video sessions), PayU (payments), Twilio SendGrid (email/SMS), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), Google Analytics & Tag Manager.
Cross-Cutting Concerns
Centralised logging, monitoring, tracing, and alerting via cloud-native tooling. GitHub Actions CI/CD. Docker containerisation.
