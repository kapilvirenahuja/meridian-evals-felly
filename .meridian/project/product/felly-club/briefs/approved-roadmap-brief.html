The Bet

Build a domain-verified mentorship marketplace for Sport, Entertainment, Business, and Entrepreneurship — four verticals abandoned by every existing platform — within 16 weeks and EUR 50K. The bet: credentialed supply + escrow-backed trust + built-in video delivery creates a defensible position in a greenfield niche before well-funded competitors notice it.

The Story

Weeks 1-6 (Foundation): Identity (E1) and Compliance (E7) ship as parallel infrastructure. No marketplace function is possible without authenticated roles and consent-compliant data handling.

Weeks 5-10 (Supply + Discovery): Mentor Onboarding (E2) populates supply; Elasticsearch-powered Search (E3) makes it discoverable. Target: 5-10 seeded mentors to unblock integration testing.

Weeks 7-12 (Core Transaction): Session Booking + Zoom SDK (E4) and Payment/Escrow (E5) close the marketplace loop. This is the riskiest block — Zoom licensing, double-booking atomics, and PayU escrow geography validation are all critical-path blockers.

Weeks 10-14 (Trust + Growth): Ratings/Disputes (E6), Communications (E8), Referral Engine (E9), and Admin Panel (E10) layer quality signals, notifications, and operational tooling on top of the working marketplace.

Assumptions

  • Domain experts in Sport/Entertainment mentor online at EUR 30-150/session price points
  • PayU supports escrow hold-and-release across India, Switzerland, USA, and Germany in a unified API — unvalidated, critical-path risk
  • Zoom SDK provides sufficient video quality for MVP; custom infrastructure not needed at launch
  • KeyCloak can be configured for multi-tenant RBAC within 16 weeks under EUR 50K budget
  • EUR 50K covers a 3-person team for 16 weeks including infrastructure and third-party services
  • 50+ mentors can be seeded via founder networks before paid acquisition is needed
  • GDPR/TPDP best-effort compliance at MVP is acceptable pending post-launch legal review
  • 10-20% commission rate is acceptable to mentors given discovery and scheduling value

Exclusions

  • Native mobile apps (React Native) — responsive web only for MVP
  • AI-powered mentor-mentee matching algorithms
  • Group mentorship sessions and cohort programs
  • Custom video infrastructure — Zoom SDK only
  • Multi-language UI localization beyond English
  • Marketplace for digital products (courses, templates)
  • Advanced analytics with ML predictions (churn, LTV)
  • White-label or B2B2C enterprise licensing
  • Subscription or recurring billing models
  • Automated tax calculation per jurisdiction

E1 — Identity and Access Foundation

Near P1 M High Confidence
Description
KeyCloak-based SSO, MFA, RBAC for mentee/mentor/admin personas. Foundation infrastructure every other epic depends on.
Strategic Goal
SG-01 — Launch functional MVP
Depends On
None (foundational)
Problem Today
No identity layer exists. Three personas have no accounts, authentication, or role-based access. Without this, no other platform capability can function.
Outcome After
All three personas register, authenticate with MFA, and receive role-appropriate access governed by KeyCloak RBAC. Secure token management and forced logout in place.
Strategic Connection
Hard prerequisite for all 10 epics. Multi-sided marketplace model (PP-3=5) requires role trust from day one.
In Scope
KeyCloak SSO (email/password + Google/Apple social login), TOTP MFA, RBAC (mentee/mentor/admin), session management, password reset, email verification.
Out of Scope
Mentor credentialing (E2), enterprise SSO/SAML, biometrics, WebAuthn/FIDO2, extended profile management.
Must Not Break
Foundational epic — nothing to break yet.
Success Scenarios
New user completes registration → receives verification email within 60s → logs in after verification.
Registered mentee attempts admin panel URL → receives 403 and is redirected to mentee dashboard.
User with MFA enabled enters correct credentials without TOTP → is prompted for second factor before access is granted.
Failure Conditions
User accesses role-restricted resources without holding the corresponding KeyCloak role.
Session tokens do not expire or refresh correctly — stale sessions persist beyond configured timeout.
Registration or login takes more than 5 seconds end-to-end under normal load.

E2 — Mentor Onboarding and Credentialing

Near P1 M Medium Confidence
Description
Supply-side activation engine. Mentor registration, credential submission, admin approval workflow, public profiles, and domain-verified badges across Sport/Entertainment/Business/Entrepreneurship.
Strategic Goal
SG-02 — 50 domain-verified mentors within 8 weeks of launch
Depends On
E1
Problem Today
No structured channel for domain experts in Sport/Entertainment to offer mentorship. Existing platforms ignore these verticals. Cold-start problem: no supply means no demand.
Outcome After
Mentors register, submit credentials, set rates within pricing bands, configure availability, and build public profiles with domain-verified badges. Admins manage the approval pipeline with approve/reject/request-info states.
Strategic Connection
Directly serves SG-02. Domain verification badges are the core differentiator separating Felly Club from self-reported-profile platforms. Supplies the data E3 indexes and E4 books against.
In Scope
Mentor registration with vertical selection, credential upload (S3 pre-signed URLs), admin approval state machine, public profile with domain-verified badge, pricing band enforcement, availability management.
Out of Scope
Automated credential verification, AI matching, content publishing, performance analytics (E10), bulk import tools.
Must Not Break
E1 RBAC — mentor onboarding must use existing KeyCloak roles, not create parallel auth flows.
Success Scenarios
Mentor completes registration + credential submission → application appears in admin queue within 30s with confirmation email.
Admin approves with domain verification → mentor profile becomes searchable with domain-verified badge within 5 minutes.
Approved mentor sets rate outside pricing band → system rejects the rate and shows the valid range.
Failure Conditions
Mentor profile becomes publicly visible and searchable before admin approval is granted.
Domain-verified badges displayed on profiles that have not completed credential verification.
Onboarding flow takes more than 10 minutes for a prepared applicant.

E3 — Mentor Discovery and Search

Near P1 M High Confidence
Description
Elasticsearch-powered discovery with faceted filtering by domain, rating, price, availability, language. The bridge between mentor supply and marketplace transactions.
Strategic Goal
SG-01 — End-to-end marketplace flow
Depends On
E1, E2
Problem Today
No discovery mechanism. Even with supply onboarded (E2), mentees have no way to find mentors. Existing competitors offer basic text search only — no domain-aware filtering for Sport/Entertainment.
Outcome After
Full-text search with faceted filters (vertical, sub-domain, rating, price, availability, language, country). Autocomplete in <200ms. Sort by relevance/rating/price. Responsive across desktop and mobile. Sub-500ms query response.
Strategic Connection
Without effective search, the marketplace cannot function regardless of supply. Search results trigger session booking (E4) — this epic is the demand activation layer.
In Scope
Elasticsearch index for approved profiles, full-text search, faceted filters, autocomplete, sort options, responsive search UI. Async CDC sync pipeline (profile_updated event → sync worker).
Out of Scope
AI/vector-based matching, personalized ranking, saved searches, map-based discovery, search analytics (E10).
Must Not Break
E2 mentor profiles — search index must reflect current approved mentor data within 5 minutes of any update.
Success Scenarios
Mentee searches 'sport coaching' + Sport filter + EUR 30-80 price → only approved mentors matching both criteria appear within 500ms.
Mentee types 'entre' in search bar → autocomplete suggestions including 'Entrepreneurship' and matching mentor names appear within 200ms.
50 approved mentors present, unfiltered search → results ranked by relevance with domain-verified mentors visibly boosted.
Failure Conditions
Search returns unapproved or incomplete mentor profiles.
Faceted filter counts are inaccurate — displayed count mismatches actual results.
Search latency exceeds 1 second for standard queries.

E4 — Session Booking and Video Delivery

Near P1 L High Confidence
Description
30/60-min booking with timezone-aware scheduling, Google Calendar integration, Zoom SDK in-platform video. Full session lifecycle state machine. Highest-risk technical epic in MVP.
Strategic Goal
SG-01 — Core marketplace transaction
Depends On
E1, E2, E3
Problem Today
No booking or delivery mechanism. Marketplace has supply and discovery but no checkout. Competitors lack built-in video (MentorCruise redirects to Zoom) or offer phone-only (Clarity.fm). Built-in video is a core differentiator.
Outcome After
Mentees select slots, book sessions with timezone-aware calendar invites, and join via Zoom SDK in-platform. Session lifecycle: booked → confirmed → in-progress → completed → triggers payment release (E5) and rating prompt (E6).
Strategic Connection
Core transaction of SG-01. Transforms Felly Club from a directory into a marketplace. Session state machine is shared contract that E5, E6, and E8 depend on.
In Scope
30/60-min booking, timezone-aware slot selection, Google Calendar invites, Zoom SDK in-platform video, rescheduling/cancellation policies, optional recording with mutual consent, session state machine.
Out of Scope
Group sessions, non-Google calendar integrations, custom video infrastructure, AI session notes, recurring scheduling.
Must Not Break
E1 authentication (only booked mentor/mentee can join), E2 mentor availability, E3 search context on back navigation.
Success Scenarios
Mentee selects 60-min slot → booking created with calendar invite to both parties + slot marked unavailable within 30 seconds.
Session starts in 5 minutes, mentee clicks 'Join Session' → Zoom SDK loads in-platform connecting both parties without external app.
Mentee cancels 48h before session (policy: 24h notice) → session cancelled, slot released, both parties notified.
Failure Conditions
Two mentees successfully book the same time slot with the same mentor (double-booking).
Session video fails to connect with no fallback or error guidance.
Timezone conversion errors cause sessions to display at wrong times across the four target countries.
Cancelled session transitions to completed — invalid state machine path allowed.

E5 — Payment Processing and Escrow

Near P1 L High Confidence
Description
PayU-powered payments with escrow hold-and-release, multi-currency (EUR/USD/CHF/INR), 10-20% commission, mentor payouts, refund workflows. Financial trust infrastructure.
Strategic Goal
SG-03 — Trust through escrow and financial transparency
Depends On
E1, E4
Problem Today
No payment layer. Sessions exist but no financial mechanism. Without escrow, mentees risk paying for no-shows and mentors risk delivering unpaid sessions. Competitors lack escrow entirely.
Outcome After
Funds held in escrow at booking, released to mentor on session.completed. 10-20% commission calculated and recorded. Multi-currency support. Mentor payouts on weekly/bi-weekly schedule. Refund workflows for cancellations and disputes.
Strategic Connection
Financial mechanism backing SG-03 quality guarantee. Revenue model (EUR 8-15 commission per session). Multi-currency enables four-country launch (SG-01). PCI DSS compliance via PayU serves SG-04.
In Scope
PayU card payments + local methods, escrow hold-and-release, EUR/USD/CHF/INR support, commission calculation, mentor payout scheduling, full/partial refunds, idempotent payment requests, transaction history dashboards.
Out of Scope
Subscription billing, multi-gateway failover, installment plans, ACH/SEPA direct transfer, cryptocurrency, automated tax calculation.
Must Not Break
E1 authentication (payment flows enforce authenticated sessions), E4 session lifecycle (escrow release aligned to session.completed event).
Success Scenarios
Mentee books EUR 75 session → payment charged and held in escrow, mentor sees 'paid and held', mentee receives receipt with escrow details.
Session completes (EUR 75, 15% commission) → EUR 63.75 released to mentor, EUR 11.25 recorded as platform revenue within 24 hours.
Mentee cancels within policy window → full escrowed amount refunded to original payment method within 5 business days.
Failure Conditions
Funds released from escrow before session completion event fires.
Commission calculation produces incorrect amounts.
Payment failure leaves session in booked state without payment — slot held without charge.
Mentor payouts fail silently with no notification or retry.

E7 — Compliance and Data Protection

Near P1 M Medium Confidence
Description
Multi-geography compliance built-in: GDPR consent, India TPDP, PCI DSS scope via PayU, data export/deletion, immutable audit logging. Foundation investment. Medium confidence — TPDP and PCI DSS scenarios untested.
Strategic Goal
SG-04 — Zero compliance violations in first 6 months
Depends On
E1 (consent integrated into E1 signup form)
Problem Today
Four-country operation across three regulatory regimes (GDPR, India TPDP, US state-level) with highly sensitive financial and personal data. Compliance violations can shut down operations and incur GDPR fines up to 4% of annual turnover.
Outcome After
Granular consent collection at registration, data export (JSON within 72h), right to erasure across all systems (PostgreSQL, KeyCloak, Elasticsearch, SendGrid, Zoom), immutable audit log with hash-chain tamper evidence, PCI DSS scope managed entirely through PayU gateway.
Strategic Connection
SG-04 non-negotiable. NFR-5=4 (Multi-Regime Compliance), NFR-7=4 (Highly Sensitive Data). Compliance as built-in capability is itself a competitive advantage — most competitors operate in single regulatory regimes.
In Scope
GDPR granular consent (data processing, marketing, session recording), India TPDP consent for Indian users, data export/deletion, cookie consent, immutable audit log, PCI DSS scope management via PayU (no raw card storage).
Out of Scope
Automated regulatory reporting, legal document generation, WCAG AA accessibility, SOC2/ISO 27001, standard contractual clauses for cross-border transfers.
Must Not Break
E1 signup flow (consent integrates without disrupting UX), E5 payments (PCI DSS scope must not interfere with PayU processing).
Success Scenarios
New German user at registration step → sees GDPR granular consent options (processing, marketing, recording) → can proceed only after required consents given.
Registered user requests data export → receives downloadable JSON of all personal data within 72 hours.
Admin queries audit log for specific user's consent history → sees immutable, timestamped record of all consent grants, revocations, and data access events.
Failure Conditions
User data processed without valid consent, or consent records incomplete or missing for any user in any jurisdiction.
Audit log entries can be modified or deleted — compliance trail unreliable.
Data deletion request leaves residual PII in any system component (search index, analytics, backups).
