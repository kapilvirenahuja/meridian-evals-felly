Problem Statement

The mentorship landscape is fragmented across informal networks, LinkedIn DMs, and niche platforms that rarely span beyond tech and business domains. Individuals seeking guidance in Sport, Entertainment, Entrepreneurship, and Business face several systemic friction points: 1. Discovery gap: No single marketplace aggregates verified mentors across Sport, Entertainment, Business, and Entrepreneurship verticals. Existing platforms (MentorCruise, GrowthMentor, ADPList) are tech/startup-centric. Sports mentorship is scattered across NGO programs (Living Sport, WeCOACH) and informal networks with no transactional layer. 2. Trust deficit: Mentor credentials are self-reported on most platforms. There is no standardized domain-verification process, leaving mentees unable to distinguish credentialed experts from self-proclaimed coaches. 3. Pricing opacity: Rates range from free (ADPList) to $960/hour (Clarity.fm) with no standardization. Per-minute billing (Clarity.fm) creates anxiety; flat monthly subscriptions (MentorCruise at $200-400/month) lock mentees into commitments before trust is established. 4. Payment risk: Most platforms lack escrow mechanisms. Mentors risk non-payment for completed sessions; mentees risk paying for no-shows or subpar sessions with limited recourse. 5. Multi-geography compliance: Platforms operating across India, EU (Germany), Switzerland, and USA must navigate GDPR, India's DPDP Act (formerly TPDP), PCI DSS for payments, and Germany's FernUSG (Distance Learning Protection Act) which now applies to online mentoring/coaching. 6. Session accountability: No mainstream mentorship platform offers session recordings for dispute resolution, creating a he-said/she-said dynamic when quality disputes arise.

Differentiators

Vertical breadth beyond tech: Sport and Entertainment verticals are unserved by existing marketplace platforms — Felly Club is the first curated marketplace spanning Sport, Entrepreneurship, Business, and Entertainment
Domain-verified mentors: Structured credential verification process (not self-reported) with <10% target acceptance rate, ensuring trust before first session
Escrow-backed payments: Session fees held in escrow until session completion, protecting both mentors (guaranteed payment) and mentees (recourse for no-shows or disputes)
Session recordings for dispute resolution: Video sessions can be recorded and used as evidence in quality disputes — no other mentorship marketplace offers this
Multi-geography compliance by design: Built for GDPR (EU/Germany), DPDP Act (India), Swiss data protection (nFADP), PCI DSS, and German FernUSG from day one
Per-session transactional model: No monthly lock-in subscriptions — mentees pay per session, reducing commitment friction and enabling trust-building before repeat booking
Referral-driven growth: Built-in referral program to drive organic acquisition in network-effect-dependent marketplace

Market Risks

Supply-side / Cold start HIGH

Recruiting verified mentors in Sport and Entertainment is harder than tech — these professionals are less digitally native and less accustomed to platform-mediated mentoring

Mitigation: Seed supply through partnerships with sports academies, entertainment industry guilds, and entrepreneur networks in target geographies; offer early mentors favorable commission rates
Marketplace liquidity HIGH

Two-sided marketplace chicken-and-egg problem — mentees won't join without mentors, mentors won't join without mentees

Mitigation: Launch vertical-by-vertical (Entrepreneurship first where supply is most available), concentrate on 1-2 geographies initially, use referral program to bootstrap demand
Regulatory complexity HIGH

Operating across India, Germany, Switzerland, and USA requires compliance with GDPR, DPDP Act, nFADP, PCI DSS, and Germany's FernUSG (which now applies to online mentoring). Non-compliance carries significant fines.

Mitigation: GDPR-first architecture with data residency controls; engage local legal counsel per geography; FernUSG classification review before Germany launch
Budget and timeline HIGH

EUR 125,000 fixed budget for 16-week delivery across web, iOS, Android, and admin portal is aggressive for a multi-geography marketplace with payment escrow, video integration, and regulatory compliance

Mitigation: Strict MVP scoping — defer non-essential features; use third-party services (Zoom for video, Stripe for payments) to reduce custom build; prioritize web app, defer native mobile to post-MVP if needed
Competitive response MEDIUM

MentorCruise or ADPList could add Sport/Entertainment verticals or launch in target geographies, leveraging existing network effects

Mitigation: First-mover advantage in underserved verticals; deep domain verification creates a defensible quality moat; curated approach is harder to replicate than horizontal expansion
Payment processing MEDIUM

Escrow payments across four jurisdictions involve different payment regulations, tax withholding requirements, and currency conversion complexities

Mitigation: Use Stripe Connect or equivalent with built-in multi-currency and compliance features; start with primary currencies (EUR, USD, INR, CHF) only
Mentor quality control MEDIUM

Verification of credentials in Sport and Entertainment lacks standardized credentialing bodies compared to tech certifications

Mitigation: Develop domain-specific verification rubrics; partner with industry bodies for credential validation; use session ratings as ongoing quality signal
Video platform dependency LOW

Reliance on Zoom for video conferencing creates vendor lock-in and limits control over session recording, quality, and user experience

Mitigation: Abstract video provider behind an integration layer; evaluate alternatives (Daily.co, Whereby) for future flexibility

Value Proposition

Felly Club is the first curated mentorship marketplace that spans beyond tech into Sport, Entrepreneurship, Business, and Entertainment verticals. Unlike platforms where credentials are self-reported and payment is uncertain, Felly Club verifies every mentor's domain expertise, holds session fees in escrow until completion, and records sessions for dispute resolution. Mentees get guaranteed quality and financial protection. Mentors get guaranteed payment and professional credibility. The platform operates across India, Switzerland, USA, and Germany with compliance built in from day one — not bolted on later.
