Problem

The global mentorship landscape is fragmented and opaque. Aspiring individuals struggle to identify credible, accessible mentors, while accomplished professionals lack a structured, trustworthy channel to offer guidance. Key pain points include no standardized discovery mechanism for domain-specific mentors, absence of transparent pricing and quality indicators, high friction in scheduling and payment, limited accountability and dispute resolution, and no data-driven insights for participants. The niche of Sport and Entertainment mentorship is particularly underserved — existing platforms focus heavily on tech, business, and design, leaving athletes, performers, and creative professionals without a curated marketplace for domain-verified mentors.

Differentiators

Multi-vertical curation across Sport, Entrepreneurship, Business, and Entertainment — no existing platform covers all four with domain-verified mentors
Domain-verified mentor badges with structured credentialing — goes beyond self-reported profiles used by most competitors
Built-in video session delivery (30/60 min) with optional recording — eliminates dependency on external tools like Zoom or Skype
Transparent pricing bands with platform-enforced min/max ranges and 10–20% commission — contrasts with opaque per-minute billing (Clarity.fm) or flat subscriptions (GrowthMentor)
Escrow-based payment with structured refund and dispute resolution — addresses a gap in MentorCruise, ADPList, and most competitors
Multi-geography compliance by design (GDPR, India TPDP, PCI DSS) — enables operation in India, Switzerland, USA, and Germany from day one
Sport and Entertainment verticals are a greenfield opportunity — existing platforms focus almost exclusively on tech, design, and startup advisory
Referral-driven acquisition model targeting mentee network effects within niche communities

Risks

Cold-start problem: Attracting sufficient high-quality mentors in Sport and Entertainment verticals before mentee demand materializes — chicken-and-egg marketplace challenge
Budget constraint: EUR 50K budget and 16-week timeline limits MVP scope significantly compared to well-funded competitors (ADPList raised $1.3M seed; MentorCruise is established and profitable)
Multi-geography compliance complexity: GDPR (EU/Switzerland), India TPDP, PCI DSS across four countries increases legal and engineering cost disproportionate to early-stage revenue
Mentor monetization expectations: Domain experts in Sport and Entertainment may have higher earning expectations than the platform's commission model can sustain at low volume
Video infrastructure cost: Built-in video delivery (vs. redirecting to Zoom) adds significant infrastructure cost and reliability burden for an MVP
Trust and safety: Recording mentorship sessions raises privacy concerns and may deter mentors, especially high-profile individuals in Entertainment and Sport
Market education: Mentees in Sport and Entertainment may not yet associate online platforms with mentorship — demand generation requires category creation, not just capture
Competition from free alternatives: ADPList's free model and SCORE's volunteer network set low price anchors that complicate willingness-to-pay for a new entrant
Regulatory variance: Payment processing (escrow, cross-border payouts, refunds) across India, Switzerland, USA, and Germany involves multiple payment rails and regulatory regimes

Value Proposition

Vision: To establish Felly Club as the premier tech-enabled mentorship ecosystem for Sport, Entrepreneurship, Business, and Entertainment — connecting aspiring individuals with domain-verified mentors through a transparent, trust-driven marketplace. Felly Club bridges the gap between raw ambition and structured guidance by delivering curated discovery, frictionless scheduling, secure payments, and quality-assured sessions across four countries, all within a EUR 50K budget and 16-week delivery window.

For Ambitious Mentees: Discover credible, domain-verified mentors in Sport, Entertainment, Business, and Entrepreneurship — verticals ignored by existing platforms. Book sessions with transparent pricing, built-in video delivery, and quality guarantees backed by ratings, refund policies, and dispute resolution. No more guessing if a mentor is legitimate or overpaying through opaque billing models.

For Domain-Expert Mentors: Monetize your expertise through a structured channel that handles scheduling, payments, and compliance. Receive domain-verified badges that signal credibility, set your own rates within transparent pricing bands, and get reliable payouts with escrow protection. Focus on mentoring — the platform handles the operational overhead.

For Platform Administrators: Manage a multi-geography mentorship marketplace with purpose-built tooling for mentor curation, dispute resolution, financial operations, and compliance monitoring. Role-based access, audit trails, and dashboard analytics reduce manual oversight and enable confident decision-making across India, Switzerland, USA, and Germany.

Assumptions

Domain experts in Sport and Entertainment are willing to mentor through an online platform at price points of EUR 30–150 per session
Mentees in Sport and Entertainment verticals will pay for structured mentorship when discovery, credentialing, and quality assurance reduce uncertainty
Zoom SDK integration provides sufficient video quality and reliability for MVP — custom video infrastructure is not needed at launch
PayU supports escrow-like hold-and-release payment flows across India, Switzerland, USA, and Germany without requiring a full escrow banking license
KeyCloak can be configured for multi-tenant RBAC with social login within the 16-week timeline without excessive customization
EUR 50K budget is sufficient for a 3-person engineering team over 16 weeks, covering infrastructure, third-party service costs, and contingency
Initial mentor supply (50+ mentors) can be seeded through founder networks and targeted outreach before paid acquisition is needed
GDPR and India TPDP compliance can be achieved at MVP level through consent management and data handling policies without dedicated legal counsel on retainer
Responsive web application provides adequate mobile experience for MVP — native mobile apps are not required for initial market validation
A 10–20% platform commission rate is competitive and acceptable to mentors given the value of discovery, scheduling, payment, and quality infrastructure
